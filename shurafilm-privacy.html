Shura Film Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026. Covers Shura Film: 35mm Film Camera, Android package com.shurastudios.shurafilm, version 1.0.0 (build 2) and later versions with the same features.

Shura Film is a camera. Your photos are processed on your phone and saved to your phone's gallery. There is no account, no advertising, no in-app purchase, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, and no Shura Studios server. The app does not request internet access.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only while the app is open on the camera screen, to show the viewfinder and take the picture you ask for. The film look, border and date stamp are applied on the device. Finished photos are saved as JPEG files in your device's Pictures/Shura Film folder, where your gallery and other apps you allow can see them. Photos are never uploaded by Shura Film. They leave your device only if you choose Share and pick another app, which then handles them under its own policy.

Shura Film does not record location in photos and does not request location access. The in-app album lists only the photos Shura Film itself saved; it does not read the rest of your photo library.

Information stored in the app

Your chosen camera body, lens and film, pinned favourites, the frame counter of the current roll and the flash setting are kept in Android app-private storage. Android may include these settings in your device backup according to your device and Google Account backup settings; Shura Film does not access your Google Account or Drive.

Google Play purchase

Shura Film is a paid app. You buy it once on Google Play and every camera, lens and film is included: there are no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads. Google handles the payment, refunds and your Google Account under its own terms and the Google Privacy Policy; Shura Studios receives sales reports from Google but never your card details or password. For refund questions, use Google Play refund support.

Permissions

Camera takes the photos. Storage (Android 9 and older only) saves them into the Pictures folder; newer Android versions need no storage permission. Shura Film does not request internet access, and does not request your microphone, contacts, location, messages or access to your full photo library.

Support

If you email us, we receive the address and whatever you choose to send, use it only to answer you, keep it only as long as needed for support or legal obligations, and never sell it. Please don't send passwords or card details.

Your choices and retention

Delete any photo from the in-app album or your gallery. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling removes its settings; photos already saved to your gallery stay there until you delete them. Shura Studios cannot see, retrieve or erase anything on your device.

Children

Shura Film is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13. It does not ask anyone for personal information or an account. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support address, contact us and we will delete it.

Future changes

If a later release adds accounts, sharing to a feed, advertising, analytics or a server, this policy and the Play Store data-safety disclosures will be updated before those features are enabled.
