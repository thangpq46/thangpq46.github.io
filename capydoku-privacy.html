Capydoku Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026. Covers Capydoku: Cozy Logic Puzzle, Android package com.shurastudios.capydoku, version 1.0.0 and later versions with the same features.

Puzzles work offline and your progress is stored on your device. The game shows ads from Google AdMob and offers optional coin packs through Google Play. Capydoku has no studio account, chat, or Shura Studios backend.

Information on your device

Capydoku stores your cleared levels, stars, coins, collected capybaras, garden decorations, daily-puzzle streak, settings, whether ads between levels are removed, and the Google Play purchase tokens of coin packs already credited. These records let you resume your game and stop the same purchase from crediting coins twice. They stay in Android app-private storage and are not uploaded to a Shura Studios server. The game does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

Advertising

Capydoku shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve, cap and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device and app information, diagnostics, and how you interact with ads. Google handles this data under its own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device's Google settings.

Google Play purchases

Coin packs are optional consumable purchases made through Google Play Billing. Any coin pack also removes the ads between levels. Google handles payment methods and billing under its own terms and privacy policy; Capydoku never receives your card or bank details or your Google Account password. Google Play gives the app the product, purchase token and purchase state, which the game uses on your device to credit the pack once and then consume the purchase. Pending or canceled payments do not grant coins. For billing or refund questions, use Google Play refund support or contact us.

Permissions

Internet and network-state access are used for ads and Google Play Billing. The billing permission lets the app talk to Google Play. Vibration supports game feedback. The advertising ID permission is used by the AdMob SDK. Capydoku does not request your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, messages or location.

Your choices and retention

Settings → Reset progress in the game erases your game progress but keeps ad removal and the record of credited purchases, so paid features are not lost. Clearing Capydoku's app storage or uninstalling the app erases everything, including coins and ad removal. Android may back up and restore app data according to your device and Google Account settings. Shura Studios cannot retrieve or erase local saves remotely.

Children

Capydoku is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's personal information was sent to our support address, contact us and we will delete it.

Support

If you email us, we receive the address and information you choose to send. We use it only to answer your request, keep it only as long as needed for support or legal obligations, and never sell it.

Future changes

If a later release adds accounts, analytics or server-based storage, this policy and the Play Store disclosures will be updated before those features are enabled. Changes are posted at this address with a new date.
