Geni Fighter Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026. Covers Geni Fighter, Android package com.shurastudios.genifighter, version 1.0.0 and later versions with the same features.

Geni Fighter shows rewarded ads through Google AdMob, sells optional in-game gem packs through Google Play, and uses Unity Gaming Services for an anonymous player ID, cloud save, leaderboards, analytics and crash reports. It does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

Information on your device

Your game profile (progress, heroes, gems, energy, purchases credited), a backup copy of it, and your settings (language, volume, graphics quality, notification and review preferences) are stored in app-private storage. The profile is signed to detect tampering. Local "energy full" reminders are scheduled on your device if you allow notifications.

Unity Gaming Services

The game uses Unity Gaming Services (UGS), operated by Unity Technologies:

  • Authentication — the game signs you in anonymously and receives a random player ID. No email or social login is used.
  • Cloud Save — your game profile is stored against that player ID so progress can be restored.
  • Leaderboards — your endless-mode score and the player name you type in are uploaded and shown to other players. Do not use your real name or anything personal as a player name.
  • Analytics — gameplay and purchase events (for example level reached, items bought), device model, OS version and coarse region. Analytics starts only after the consent choice described below allows it.
  • Cloud Diagnostics — crash and error reports with technical device information, so we can fix bugs.

Unity processes this data under the Unity Privacy Policy. To delete your UGS data, email us with your leaderboard name and roughly when you last played, and we will delete it.

Advertising

Geni Fighter shows rewarded ads that you choose to watch, served by Google AdMob. AdMob may process your device's advertising ID, IP-derived coarse location, device and app information to serve and measure ads, under the Google Privacy & Terms. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK) a Google-certified consent form is shown first, and you can change your choice later from the game's settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Purchases

Gem packs, the starter pack and the monthly card are optional consumable purchases through Google Play Billing (via Unity IAP). Google handles payment details; we never receive your card number or Google password. The game receives the purchase token and product information to credit your items once. A Google-hosted check of the purchase token may be used to verify that a purchase is genuine.

Other network requests

The game downloads its economy configuration from our website and makes a time check against a Google endpoint. These requests contain no personal information beyond the technical data any web request carries (such as your IP address). Google Play In-App Review and In-App Update may be shown through Google Play.

Permissions

Internet and network state (online features, ads, purchases), notifications (optional energy reminders), vibration (haptic feedback), billing (Google Play purchases) and the advertising ID (AdMob). Geni Fighter does not request camera, microphone, contacts, photos, storage or location.

Children

Geni Fighter is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information (for example in a leaderboard name), contact us and we will remove it.

Retention and your choices

Local data stays until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Cloud Save, leaderboard and analytics data are kept while your player ID is in use and deleted on request. You can refuse personalised ads in the consent form, and you can ask us for access to or deletion of your data at any time.

Support

If you email us, we receive your address and what you choose to send, and use it only to answer you.

Changes

If we change this policy we update the effective date above; material changes are announced in the game before they take effect.
