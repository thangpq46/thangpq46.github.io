ShuDraw Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026. Covers ShuDraw: Learn to Draw, Android package com.shura.studios.draw, version 1.0.0 and later versions with the same features.

ShuDraw collects no personal data. It has no ads, no purchases, no account, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it does not use the internet. Everything stays on your device.

Information on your device

Your lesson progress, streak, chosen interests and level, and the drawings you save to your journal (photos you take or choose, and drawings you make on screen) are stored in the app's private storage. Android may include this data in your device backup or device transfer, under your Google Account settings. We never receive it.

Camera and photos

To photograph your drawing, ShuDraw opens your device's camera app, which saves the picture straight into ShuDraw's private journal. To use an existing picture, ShuDraw opens Android's photo picker, which gives the app only the image you choose. The app requests no camera, storage or media permission and cannot browse your photos.

Things you choose to share

When you tap Share on a journal drawing, the image goes to the app you pick. The privacy policy link opens in your browser.

Permissions

None. ShuDraw does not request internet, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, location or notification permissions.

Children

ShuDraw is a general-audience app. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Retention and deletion

Delete any drawing from the journal with its Delete button. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes all of its data.

Changes

If we change this policy we update the effective date above, and we describe material changes in the app's release notes before they take effect.
