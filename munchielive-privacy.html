Munchie Live Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026. Covers Munchie Live, Android package com.shurastudios.munchielive, version 1.0.0 and later versions with the same features.

Munchie Live works fully offline and needs no account. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and does not request internet access. Your progress is stored only on your device. Shura Studios does not collect, share or sell any personal information from this game.

Information stored on your device

Munchie Live stores your level, experience points, in-game gold and owned outfits in Android app-private storage so you can continue where you stopped. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to Shura Studios or anyone else. The viewers, chat messages and gifts shown in the game are simulated by the app; they are not real people.

Android may back up and restore app data according to your device and Google Account backup settings. The operating system manages that backup; Munchie Live does not access your Google Account or Google Drive files.

Permissions

Munchie Live does not request internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, messages or any other sensitive permission.

Children

Munchie Live is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. It does not ask players to enter personal information, create an account or communicate with other players. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Retention and your choices

Data on your device stays until you clear Munchie Live's app storage or uninstall the game; Shura Studios cannot read or erase it remotely. If you email us, we use your message only to answer you, keep it only as long as needed for support or legal obligations, and you can ask us to delete it.

Changes

If a later version adds ads, purchases, accounts, analytics, cloud saves or other data collection, this policy and the Google Play data safety section will be updated before those features are enabled.
