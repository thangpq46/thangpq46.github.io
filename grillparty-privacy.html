Grill Party Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026. Covers Grill Party: Skewer Sort, Android package com.shurastudios.grillparty, version 1.0.0 and later versions with the same features.

Gameplay works offline and needs no account. The game shows ads from Google AdMob and offers optional purchases through Google Play. Your progress, coins and settings are stored on your device. Shura Studios does not run a server for this game and does not sell personal information.

Information stored on your device

Grill Party stores your level, coins, lives, boosters, daily-gift progress, sound, music and vibration settings, and a list of Google Play transaction identifiers that have already been credited, in Android app-private storage. This data lets you continue where you stopped and prevents one purchase from being credited twice. It is not uploaded to Shura Studios.

Android may back up and restore app data according to your device and Google Account backup settings. The operating system manages that backup; Grill Party does not access your Google Account or Google Drive files.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Grill Party shows ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob): full-screen ads between some levels and optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch for a bonus. Players who buy Remove Ads no longer see ads between levels; rewarded ads stay optional.

To serve, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and operating system details, app interaction and ad interaction data, and approximate location derived from the IP address. Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy and its partner-sites policy. Data is encrypted in transit.

Where the law requires consent (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and certain US states), the game first shows Google's consent message, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy Choices when that option is offered. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The Shop uses Google Play Billing to show localized prices and to open Google Play checkout only when you choose a product: Remove Ads (one-time) and coin packs (consumable). Google handles payment details under its own terms and privacy policy; Grill Party never receives card numbers, bank details or your Google Account password. Google Play gives the app purchase information such as the product, purchase token, transaction identifier, purchase state and time, which the game uses on your device to credit the product once and to confirm the purchase with Google Play. Remove Ads can be restored with the same Google Account through Shop → Restore purchases. Consumed coins cannot be restored after you clear app data or uninstall the game. For refunds use Google Play refund support or contact us.

Permissions

Internet and network-state access are used for ads and Google Play Billing. The billing permission allows communication with Google Play. The advertising ID permission lets the ads SDK read the advertising ID. Vibration is used for short haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings. Grill Party does not request your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, messages or precise location.

Children

Grill Party is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. It does not ask players to enter personal information, create an account or communicate with other players. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Retention and your choices

Data on your device stays until you clear Grill Party's app storage or uninstall the game; Shura Studios cannot read or erase it remotely. Google retains advertising and transaction data under its own policies. If you email us, we use your message only to answer you, keep it only as long as needed for support or legal obligations, and you can ask us to delete it.

Changes

If a later version adds accounts, analytics, cloud saves or other data collection, this policy and the Google Play data safety section will be updated before those features are enabled.
