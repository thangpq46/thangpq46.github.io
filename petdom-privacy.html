PetDom Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026. Covers PetDom: Pocket Pixels, Android package com.shura.studios.pets.dom, version 2.2.0 and later versions with the same features.

Gameplay works offline. Coin packs are optional consumable purchases through Google Play. PetDom has no studio account, advertising, analytics SDK, chat, or Shura Studios backend. Your game progress and coin balance are stored on your device.

Information on your device

PetDom stores your level, stars, earned and purchased coins, boosters, adopted pets, unfinished board, sound preference, and a record of credited purchases in Android app-private storage. These records support saving and resuming your game and prevent the same purchase from crediting coins twice. The purchase record stores a SHA-256 digest of each credited Google Play purchase token, together with your saved game; the raw token and payment-card details are not stored in the game save. They are not uploaded to a Shura Studios server.

Android may back up and restore app data according to your device and Google Account backup settings. The operating system manages that backup; PetDom does not access your Google Account or Google Drive files. Backup availability depends on your device settings and is not guaranteed. Shura Studios does not operate a cloud wallet or offer account-based balance recovery.

Google Play services

The Coin Shop uses Google Play Billing to request available products and localized prices and to open Google Play checkout only when you choose a coin pack. Google handles payment methods and billing under its own terms and privacy policy. PetDom does not receive payment-card numbers, bank details, or your Google Account password.

Google Play provides purchase information to the app, including the product, purchase token, purchase state, quantity, package, purchase time, order information when available, and a digital signature. PetDom processes this information on your device to verify the signature, credit the correct coin pack once, and consume the completed purchase through Google Play. The app exchanges billing requests and purchase tokens with Google Play to complete and check transactions. It does not send purchase records or game saves to a Shura Studios server.

Pending or canceled payments do not grant coins. On opening or resuming the app, or when you tap Check purchases, PetDom checks for unfinished purchases with Google Play. Once coins are saved locally, the app consumes the purchase so that the pack can be bought again. Keep the app installed and reopen it with an internet connection to finish a paid purchase.

Coin packs are consumable, with no subscription or recurring charge. Once a purchase is consumed, Google Play does not restore its coins as an unspent balance. Clearing app storage, uninstalling, losing a device, or moving to another device can lose coins and progress. Android backup may restore some app data but is not a guaranteed recovery method. For billing or refund questions, use Google Play refund support or contact us. Without a server, PetDom does not automatically reconcile refunds of already consumed coins across devices.

Google Play and its billing components may process account, device, service, and diagnostic information under the Google Privacy Policy. Google operates these services. PetDom does not include an advertising SDK or a separate analytics or crash-reporting service.

Permissions

Network and network-state access support Google Play Billing. The billing permission permits communication with Google Play. Vibration permission supports game feedback. PetDom does not request your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, messages, or location.

Support

If you email us, we receive the address and information you choose to send. We use that information to answer your request, retain it only as needed for support or legal obligations, and do not sell it. Avoid sending passwords, card details, or other sensitive information. Contact shurastudios@gmail.com for questions or to request deletion of a support conversation.

Your choices and retention

Your progress stays on your device until you clear PetDom's app storage or remove the app. Those actions can erase your progress, purchased coin balance, and local purchase ledger. Unfinished purchases may be queried from the same Google Play account before consumption; consumed balances cannot be reconstructed by Shura Studios. Google retains its transaction records under its own retention policies. Android backup copies are controlled through your device and Google Account settings. Shura Studios cannot retrieve or erase local saves remotely.

Children

PetDom does not ask players to submit personal information, create an account, or communicate with other players. If you believe personal information from a child was sent to our support address, contact us so we can address the request.

Future changes

If a later release introduces accounts, advertising, analytics, or server-based coin storage, this policy and the relevant Play Store disclosures will be updated before those features are enabled.
