SHURA STUDIOS · PETDOM

PetDom Privacy Policy

Effective 10 November 2026. Covers PetDom: Pocket Pixels, Android package com.shura.studios.pets.dom, version 2.1.1 (closed testing).

Gameplay works offline. This version has no advertising, analytics SDK, user accounts, chat, or enabled real-money purchases. Shura Studios does not receive your game progress.

Information on your device

PetDom stores your level, stars, earned coins, boosters, adopted pets, unfinished board, and sound preference in Android app-private storage. These records support saving and resuming your game. They are not uploaded to a Shura Studios server.

Android may back up and restore app data according to your device and Google Account backup settings. The operating system manages that backup; PetDom does not access your Google Account or Google Drive files. Backup availability depends on your device settings.

Google Play services

The Coin Shop uses Google Play Billing to request available coin products and prices for your market. Checkout is disabled in this version. PetDom does not receive payment-card details or launch a payment transaction.

Google Play and its billing components may process account, device, service, and diagnostic information under the Google Privacy Policy. Google operates these services. PetDom does not include an advertising SDK or a separate analytics or crash-reporting service.

Permissions

Network and network-state access support Google Play Billing. The billing permission permits communication with Google Play. Vibration permission supports game feedback. PetDom does not request your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, messages, or location.

Support

If you email us, we receive the address and information you choose to send. We use that information to answer your request, retain it only as needed for support or legal obligations, and do not sell it. Avoid sending passwords, card details, or other sensitive information. Contact shurastudios@gmail.com for questions or to request deletion of a support conversation.

Your choices and retention

Your progress stays on your device until you clear PetDom's app storage or remove the app. Those actions can erase your progress. Android backup copies are controlled through your device and Google Account settings. Shura Studios cannot retrieve or erase local saves remotely.

Children

PetDom does not ask players to submit personal information, create an account, or communicate with other players. If you believe personal information from a child was sent to our support address, contact us so we can address the request.

Future changes

If a later release introduces purchases, accounts, or server-based coin storage, this policy and the relevant Play Store disclosures will be updated before those features are enabled.
