ShuraPS2 Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026. Covers ShuraPS2, Android package com.shura.emulator.ps2, version 1.3.2 and later versions with the same features.

ShuraPS2 works entirely offline. It has no internet permission, no ads, no analytics or crash reporting, no purchases and no account. Nothing you load or play is sent to us or anyone else.

Your files

You choose game files and BIOS files with Android's file picker or by granting "All files access", which lets the app find and open the games and BIOS you keep on your device. The app only reads files to list and run them. It does not upload, share or modify your files outside its own folders. ShuraPS2 does not include any games or BIOS; you are responsible for using only files you own.

Information on your device

Copies of BIOS files you import, save states, memory cards, screenshots, custom GPU drivers, the emulator's data folder and your settings are stored in app-private storage. Android may include app data in your device backup according to your Google Account settings.

Permissions

  • All files access / read storage — to find and open your game and BIOS files.
  • Vibration — touch feedback and controller rumble.
  • Wake lock — keeps the screen on while you play.

Bluetooth and USB controllers work through Android's standard input system without extra permissions. The app does not request internet, camera, microphone, contacts or location.

Children

ShuraPS2 collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Support

If you email us, we receive your address and what you choose to send, and use it only to answer you.

Changes

If a future version adds network features, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
