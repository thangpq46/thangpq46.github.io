How we work

Six rules we don't break

These aren't marketing lines. They are the constraints we design against, and they are what the privacy policy and terms on this site actually commit us to.

On the device by default

Your diary entries, your saved birds, your settings: written to private app storage. Nothing leaves the phone unless a feature you switched on has to send it, and the policy says exactly which ones do.

No account, ever

We don't run a login server, so there is no profile of you to leak, subpoena or sell. The only sign-in anywhere in the catalogue is optional Google Drive backup in Kuro — and that is your Drive, not ours.

Honest monetisation

Ads and an optional one-time or subscription upgrade, priced up front through Google Play. No countdown pressure, no fake discounts, no subscription you have to email us to escape — you cancel in Play, like everything else.

Kids' apps stay clean

Shura Kids Piano follows Google Play's Families policy: no advertising ID, no location permissions, no analytics profile, and ads restricted to general-audience content only.

Legal you can actually read

One privacy policy and one terms of service for the whole catalogue, written in plain sentences, with a per-app table so you can see at a glance what each one touches.

A human answers

Support goes to one mailbox and the people who wrote the code read it. Bug report, refund question, data request — you get a person, not a ticket robot.

Get in touch

Something broken, or just curious?

Write to us about a bug, a refund, a translation that reads wrong, or a request to see and delete what an app holds about you. We read everything that arrives.
