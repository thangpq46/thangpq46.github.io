Pets Jump Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026. Covers Pets Jump, Android package com.shura.studios.pets.jump, version 1.2.0 and later versions with the same features.

Pets Jump shows ads through Google AdMob, sells optional gem packs and upgrades through Google Play, and uses Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config. A short age question on first launch switches on stricter settings for players under 13. There is no Shura Studios account or server.

Information on your device

Your progress, gems, purchases credited (including purchase tokens), settings, the age range you selected, and a random 8-character player ID generated by the app are stored in app-private storage. Android may include this data in your device backup or device transfer, under your Google Account settings.

Age question and younger players

On first launch the game asks a neutral age question. If you indicate you are under 13: ads are requested as child-directed and under the age of consent, with personalised ads off and ad content limited to the G rating; Firebase Analytics is turned off. Crash reports (below) are still collected so we can fix crashes.

Advertising

Pets Jump shows rewarded and interstitial ads through Google AdMob. For players 13 and older, AdMob may use the advertising ID, device and app information and IP-derived coarse location to serve and measure ads, under the Google Privacy & Terms. Where required, a Google-certified consent form is shown before ads load, and you can reopen your privacy choices from the game's settings. The "No forced ads" purchase removes interstitial ads.

Firebase

Firebase Analytics (players 13+ only, following your consent choice) records gameplay events, virtual-currency earned and spent, and answers to an optional three-question survey, together with an app-instance ID, device model, OS version and coarse region. You can reset analytics from the settings screen. Firebase Crashlytics sends crash reports (stack trace, device model, OS version, app state) for all players. Firebase Remote Config downloads game tuning values. Google processes this data under the Firebase privacy terms.

Purchases

Gem packs are consumable; "No forced ads" and the starter pack are one-time purchases. All run through Google Play Billing; Google handles payment details and we never receive card numbers. The app stores the purchase token locally to credit items once and restore one-time purchases.

Google Play Games

If Google Play Games is available, the game may sign you in automatically to submit scores to leaderboards. Your Play Games profile name and score are handled by Google under its own privacy policy.

Things you choose to share

Sharing text, exporting a backup file, or sending a friend challenge link only happens when you use those features, and goes to the app you pick. If you use "Send feedback", the email draft includes your player ID, device model, Android version and last level so we can reproduce problems; you can edit it before sending.

Permissions

Internet and network state (ads, purchases, Firebase, time check), notifications (optional local reminders), vibration (haptics) and, through AdMob, the advertising ID. No camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.

Retention and your choices

Local data stays until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Firebase data is kept under Google's retention settings. Contact us for access or deletion requests.

Changes

If we change this policy we update the effective date above; material changes are announced in the game before they take effect.
