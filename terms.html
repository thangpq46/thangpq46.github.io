08 Identification results are estimates

Tengu's photo and sound identification is produced by machine-learning models. A result is a probabilistic suggestion with a confidence score, not a verified fact. Do not rely on an identification where being wrong would matter: scientific research or published records, legal or regulatory compliance, protected-species reporting, environmental assessment, or any decision about handling a bird.
